Work in Academia

I have studied the interannual variability of the Argentine Sea circulation within the broader context of climate variability, and applied tools from dynamical systems theory to investigate ocean circulation. These interests run through my publications and ongoing collaborations, combining data-driven analysis with theory-informed modelling to extract robust, physically consistent insights.
