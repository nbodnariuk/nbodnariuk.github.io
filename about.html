About Me

I earned my undergraduate degree in Physical Oceanography from the Universidad Nacional del Sur (Argentina, 2015) and completed my PhD in Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences at the Universidad de Buenos Aires (2023), supported by a CONICET fellowship.

I later carried out postdoctoral research at both the CIMA (Centro de Investigación del Mar y la Atmósfera, Buenos Aires) and the LMD (Laboratoire de Météorologie Dynamique, CNRS, Paris, France). My academic work has focused on the analysis of large geophysical datasets (spanning terabytes) integrating concepts from dynamical systems theory, topology, and statistics.

In addition to my scientific background, I have professional experience in the private sector, where I have worked with meteorological databases and Monte Carlo simulations.
