Nicolás Bodnariuk

I’m a data scientist and physical oceanographer with a strong background in applied mathematics and data analysis. My experience focuses on extracting meaning from complex, high-dimensional datasets.

I’m interested in how mathematical and computational methods — from dynamical systems theory to machine learning — can be used to uncover hidden structures, model nonlinear processes, and deepen our understanding of complex systems.

I have experience handling and analysing large geophysical and meteorological datasets through my work at CONICET (the Argentinian National Research Council), CNRS (the French National Research Centre), and in the private sector.
