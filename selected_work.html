Selected Work

My work bridges mathematical modelling, data science, and geophysical fluid dynamics. Below are selected examples that illustrate how analytical and computational methods can be used to uncover structure, dynamics, and coherence in complex systems.

  • Topology meets Dynamics: Using Topological Data Analysis to characterize attractors in nonlinear ocean circulation models. This approach helps reveal how large-scale flow structures emerge from the underlying equations.
  • Pattern Discovery: Applying Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOFs), Complex EOFs, Wavelet Transforms, and Singular Spectrum Analysis to extract dominant spatiotemporal modes from terabyte-scale oceanic and atmospheric datasets.
  • Applied Modelling: Developing reduced-order models for the wind-driven double-gyre circulation to study nonlinear transitions, bifurcations, and the role of physical forcing in shaping large-scale variability.
  • Real-world Analytics: Experience in the private sector applying data-driven modelling and statistical analysis to meteorological databases used for pollutant dispersion and environmental risk assessment.
