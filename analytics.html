Data Analytics and Modelling

This section presents examples of data-driven analysis and modelling approaches applied to complex dynamical systems. The objective is to extract structure, coherence, and hidden patterns from high-dimensional datasets — using tools that range from classical spectral methods to modern machine learning and topological data analysis.

The combination of physical understanding and data science methods allows for the interpretation of nonlinear processes across scales, both in model simulations and observational datasets.
