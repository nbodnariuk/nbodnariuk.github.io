Private Sector

Selected work carried out in industrial and consulting contexts, applying mathematical modelling, uncertainty quantification, and environmental analytics to real-world data challenges.

Meteorological Station Analysis for Industry

Data curation and quality control of meteorological station records to support flare-based pollutant dispersion modelling. The process delivered validated, bias-checked datasets ready for regulatory-grade simulations.

  • Consistency, range, and step-change checks; detection of spikes and flatlines.
  • Generation of wind roses and stability class estimations for model inputs.
  • Audit-traceable ETL pipelines ensuring reproducibility and compliance.

Statistical Analysis for Heat Exchanger Performance

Statistical exploration and regression-based modelling of process data to identify operating regimes optimising heat dissipation and to detect conditions leading to suboptimal performance.

  • Feature engineering of flow, ΔT, and fouling indicators for predictive analysis.
  • ANOVA and regression techniques to quantify drivers of thermal efficiency.
  • Control charts and change-point detection for early degradation signals.

Monte Carlo Propagation of Physical Uncertainties

Monte Carlo–based uncertainty propagation applied to industrial circuit models, evaluating how incomplete or uncertain physical inputs affect the final properties of interest.

  • Distribution elicitation and statistical fitting for uncertain input parameters.
  • Forward simulations incorporating correlations and scenario sampling.
  • Sensitivity analysis and uncertainty budgets supporting risk-informed design.
